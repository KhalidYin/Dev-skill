# Synthetic protocol ONC-03, v1.0

## §1 Synopsis
Phase I/II, open-label. Part A is dose escalation with Bayesian guidance. Part B includes two noncomparative expansion cohorts at the recommended dose. Across the trial up to 60 participants may be treated. All safety events are collected through 30 days after last dose.

## §4.1 Part A dose decision
Eligible participants enter sequential cohorts of three. Dose-limiting toxicity (DLT) is evaluated through Day 28 and the committee reviews all DLT-evaluable data before escalation. Participants who stop before Day 28 for reasons unrelated to a DLT may be replaced. Model details are in Appendix A.

## §4.2 Part B expansion
Two cohorts of 20 treated participants each estimate objective response with confidence intervals. Scans are at baseline and every eight weeks. Cohorts are reported separately and no formal benchmark test is planned.

## §7 Visit schedule
Part A DLT surveillance stops after the Day 21 visit. No DLT contacts or review of safety data after Day 21 are used for Part A dose decisions.

## §10 Statistical considerations
Part A dose assignment uses the decision rule in Appendix A. Part B uses all treated participants as the denominator and counts an unevaluable response as nonresponse. Cohort size is chosen for estimation precision and feasibility. Safety summaries include all treated participants.

## Appendix A model
The committee may escalate only if the posterior probability of exceeding the target toxicity rate is below 25%. The borrowing model permits dynamic borrowing from a different product with a similar mechanism. The protocol provides no exchangeability assessment. The model calibration report is referenced but was not supplied.
