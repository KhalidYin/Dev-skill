# Synthetic protocol RCT-01, v1.0

## §1 Synopsis
Phase III double-blind parallel randomized study. Two hundred participants will be randomized 1:1, stratified by baseline disease severity. The primary endpoint is change from baseline in Score S at week 24.

## §3 Objective and endpoint
The primary objective is to estimate the difference between treatment and placebo in change from baseline in Score S at week 24 in all randomized participants. Results are to reflect the assigned treatment regardless of discontinuation.

## §4 Design
The assignment system uses concealed, centrally generated blocks. Participants, investigators, and assessors are blinded. The independent pharmacist has access to assignments.

## §7 Schedule and follow-up
The week 24 Score S assessment is performed only for participants still on assigned treatment. Participants who discontinue treatment do not receive any further Score S assessment. Safety follow-up continues for 30 days.

## §10 Statistical considerations
A total of 200 participants are planned based on a two-sided 5% test, 90% power, expected difference 4, common SD 8, and allowance for incomplete assessments. The primary comparison is by assigned group in all randomized participants, using a baseline-adjusted model with disease severity as a factor. A safety summary is based on all treated participants through 30 days after last dose. The SAP is planned but not supplied.
