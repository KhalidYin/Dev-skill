# Synthetic protocol ONC-02, v1.0

## §1 Synopsis
Phase II open-label single-arm study in relapsed disease. The primary objective is to test the objective response rate (ORR) against a prespecified null response rate. Stage 1 enrolls 15 evaluable participants. Enrollment continues to Stage 2 if at least four responses are observed.

## §3 Endpoint and assessments
ORR is the proportion of treated participants with a confirmed complete or partial response by independent review. Imaging occurs at baseline and every eight weeks. Participants without an adequate postbaseline assessment are counted as nonresponders.

## §10 Statistical considerations
The null ORR is 15% based on published historical results. No supporting citation or definition of historical eligibility or response ascertainment is included. The design targets 35%. Stage 1 evaluates 15 participants. Enrollment continues to a total of 30 if at least three responses are observed; 8 total responses constitute success. Analyze all treated participants. Safety is summarized in treated participants through 30 days after last dose.
