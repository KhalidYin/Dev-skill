# Clinical Study Protocol XR-301 (excerpt)

Protocol version: 2.0 (Amendment 1), 2026-03-15
Title: A Phase 3, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Study of XR-17 in Adults with Moderate to Severe Chronic Itch Disorder

## Synopsis

- Design: multicenter, randomized 1:1, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel group; 24-week treatment period, 4-week safety follow-up.
- Treatments: XR-17 200 mg orally once daily; matching placebo.
- Sample size: approximately 420 participants (210 per arm).
- Primary endpoint: change from baseline in Weekly Itch Score (WIS, 0-10) at Week 24.
- Key secondary endpoints, in testing order: (1) WIS responder (improvement of at least 4 points from baseline) at Week 24; (2) time to first use of rescue medication through Week 24.

## 3 Objectives and Endpoints

| Role | Objective | Endpoint |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Primary | Demonstrate superiority of XR-17 over placebo on itch severity | Change from baseline in WIS at Week 24 |
| Key secondary 1 | Compare responder rates | Proportion with WIS improvement of at least 4 points at Week 24 |
| Key secondary 2 | Compare need for rescue | Time to first rescue medication use through Week 24 |
| Secondary | Compare quality of life | Change from baseline in DLQI at Week 24 |

## 3.1 Estimand for the primary endpoint

- Population: adults with moderate to severe chronic itch disorder as defined by the eligibility criteria.
- Treatment: XR-17 200 mg once daily versus placebo, each with background emollients.
- Variable: change from baseline in WIS at Week 24.
- Intercurrent events: discontinuation of study treatment, handled with a treatment-policy strategy (data collected after discontinuation are used); use of rescue medication, handled with a hypothetical strategy (data after rescue are set to missing and handled as if rescue had not been available).
- Population-level summary: difference in mean change from baseline between arms.

## 6 Schedule of Activities (summary)

WIS is collected daily in an eDiary. Weekly scores are derived for Weeks 1-24. Clinic visits: Screening, Day 1 (baseline), Weeks 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, and follow-up at Week 28. DLQI is collected at Day 1 and Weeks 12 and 24. Rescue medication use is recorded in the eDiary and the CRF.

## 9 Safety

Adverse events are collected from informed consent through Week 28. A treatment-emergent AE (TEAE) is an AE with onset on or after the first dose and up to 28 days after the last dose. AEs are coded with MedDRA, and severity is graded by the investigator (mild, moderate, severe).

## 10 Statistical Considerations

### 10.1 Sample size

Assuming a between-arm difference of 1.0 point in mean WIS change at Week 24, an SD of 2.8, a two-sided alpha of 0.05, and 90% power, 166 evaluable participants per arm are needed. Allowing for 20% dropout, approximately 420 participants (210 per arm) will be randomized.

### 10.2 Randomization

Participants are randomized 1:1 through IWRS, stratified by region (North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific) and baseline WIS (less than 8, 8 or more).

### 10.3 Analysis sets

- Full Analysis Set (FAS): all randomized participants, analyzed as randomized.
- Per-Protocol Set (PPS): FAS participants without major protocol deviations affecting the primary endpoint and with at least 80% study-drug compliance through Week 24.
- Safety Set (SS): all participants who received at least one dose, analyzed as treated.

### 10.4 Primary analysis

The primary endpoint is analyzed in the FAS with a mixed model for repeated measures (MMRM) that includes treatment, visit, treatment-by-visit interaction, region, and baseline WIS category as fixed effects, and baseline WIS as a covariate.

### 10.5 Multiplicity

The type I error rate is controlled at a two-sided 0.05 level with a fixed-sequence procedure: primary endpoint, then key secondary 1, then key secondary 2.

### 10.6 Interim analysis

One interim analysis for futility only will be performed when 50% of participants have completed Week 24. The futility rule is non-binding and no alpha will be spent. An independent DMC will review unblinded results.
